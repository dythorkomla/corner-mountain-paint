In-Store Services

The paint side of the job, handled

We keep this store focused on one thing and do it properly: getting you the right paint, in the right colour and finish, with the tools to apply it. Here is what that means at the counter.

At the counter

What we do in store

In store

Colour matching

Bring a chip, a painted sample, or an object. We read it on the spectrophotometer and fine-tune by eye — including matches to discontinued and other-brand colours.

In store

Tinting

Every Benjamin Moore base and sheen, mixed and shaken while you wait. Consistent batches when you come back for more.

In store

Sample pots

Small tint-able samples so you can paint a test patch and live with it before buying gallons.

In store

Painting supplies & sundries

Brushes, rollers and sleeves, trays, tape, drop sheets, fillers, caulk, sandpaper, and cleanup gear — stocked and ready.

In store

Quantity estimating

Give us your wall area and coats and we’ll work out how much paint and primer to buy — so you’re not short on a Sunday or stuck with extra.

In store

Colour chips & fan decks

The full Colour Preview Studio to browse, chips to take home, and fan decks to borrow for a bigger project.

For contractors

Painting for a living?

Talk to us about contractor pricing, keeping your regular colours and bases on hand, and having orders tinted and ready for pickup so you’re not waiting at the counter with a crew on the clock.
