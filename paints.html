Paints & Stains

The Benjamin Moore range, tinted in store

As an authorized Benjamin Moore retailer, we carry the coatings the brand is known for — from the everyday to the ultra-premium — and tint them on site in the base and sheen your project calls for. If a line or size isn’t on the shelf, we can usually order it in.

Interior

Interior paint

  • Aura

    Ultra-premium

    Benjamin Moore’s top interior line: exceptional coverage, deep colour, and a washable film that stands up to repeated cleaning. The choice when you want it done once, done best.

  • Regal Select

    Premium

    A long-standing favourite for living spaces — easy to apply, self-priming on most surfaces, and available from Ulti-Matte through Eggshell, Pearl, and Satin.

  • ben

    Everyday premium

    Forgiving, low-spatter, and quick to recoat — a strong pick for bedrooms, rentals, and weekend rooms where you want a clean result without fuss.

  • ADVANCE

    Trim, doors & cabinets

    A waterborne alkyd that flows out like an oil paint and cures hard and smooth. Built for trim, doors, railings, and kitchen cabinetry.

  • Waterborne Ceiling Paint

    Ceilings

    An ultra-flat finish that hides imperfections and resists spatter — goes on pink, dries bright white so you can see your coverage.

  • Specialty finishes

    Ask in store

    Century soft-touch matte, and problem-solvers for bathrooms, kitchens, and high-traffic halls. Tell us the room and we’ll point you to the right one.

Exterior & wood

Exterior coatings & stains

  • Aura Exterior

    Ultra-premium exterior

    Colour that holds up to Alberta sun and wind, with a wide temperature application window for our short painting season.

  • Regal Select Exterior

    Premium exterior

    A dependable house paint for siding, trim, and fences — flat, low-lustre, and soft-gloss.

  • ARBORCOAT

    Exterior wood stain

    Deck and fence stain in a full range of opacities — clear, translucent, semi-transparent, and solid — so you can keep the grain or cover it.

  • Fresh Start primers

    Primer & sealer

    The right primer for bare wood, drywall, stains, and problem surfaces — the step that makes the topcoat last.

Finishes

Matching the sheen to the room

The finish changes how a colour reads and how the wall wears. Here is the short version — bring your project to the counter and we’ll narrow it down with you.

Interior sheens, flattest to glossiest
FinishWhere it worksNotes
Flat / MatteCeilings, low-traffic adult bedrooms, feature wallsHides surface flaws best; least scrubbable
EggshellLiving rooms, dining rooms, hallwaysThe everyday wall finish — soft glow, wipes clean
Pearl / SatinKitchens, bathrooms, kids’ rooms, doorsMore moisture- and scuff-resistant
Semi-GlossTrim, baseboards, railings, cabinetsCrisp lines, stands up to hands and cleaning
High-GlossFront doors, accent millworkHard, reflective; shows every prep detail

Not sure which line?

Tell us the room, the surface, and how hard it gets used. That’s usually enough for us to put the right can in your hands.
