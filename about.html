About the Store

Independent, and on Main Street

Corner Mountain Paint is Pincher Creek’s authorized Benjamin Moore retailer — an independent shop that keeps professional-grade paint, real colour advice, and the right supplies in town, instead of a trip out of town.

For a job that matters — a whole house, a rental turnover, a storefront — the paint and the colour decision are worth getting right the first time. That is easier to do across a counter, with the full colour library on the wall and someone who can talk through undertones, finishes, and how much you actually need.

That is the store we run: focused on paint, stocked with the Benjamin Moore range, and staffed by people who would rather send you home with the right can than the biggest sale. Samuel Reimer looks after sales — ask for him at the counter or by email.

Why local matters

A Benjamin Moore dealer in town

Running out of paint mid-coat, discovering the colour reads wrong on the wall, needing one more roller sleeve — none of that should cost you a round trip out of town. Having a Benjamin Moore dealer in Pincher Creek keeps projects moving and keeps money local.

We’re here for homeowners, landlords, farms, and the trades.
