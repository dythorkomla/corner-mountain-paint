Colour Consultation

Bring a swatch. Leave with a plan.

Choosing colour from a two-inch chip under store light is how projects go wrong. Our in-store consultation slows that down: we work through the full Benjamin Moore colour system with you, test against your own materials, and settle the finish — so you buy the right paint once.

What we work through

Four things we settle at the counter

Undertone matching

Why a “grey” goes green on one wall and purple on another. We read the undertone against your lighting, floors, and fixed finishes so it stays the colour you meant.

Whole-room & whole-house palettes

Wall, trim, ceiling, and the next room over — chosen together so the house reads as one home instead of a set of separate decisions.

Finish selection

Matte, eggshell, satin, semi-gloss. We match the sheen to how the room gets used and how much light rakes across the wall.

Matching what you already have

An existing wall, a discontinued colour, a cushion, a cabinet door — bring it in and we match it on the spectrophotometer, then fine-tune by eye.

The colour library

The full Colour Preview Studio

Every Benjamin Moore collection is on the wall and open to browse — Classic Colours, Colour Preview, Historical Colours, Off-Whites, and the Designer Classics. Thousands of colours, organized so you can actually find your way through them.

Take chips home, live with them for a few days, and check them morning and evening before you commit. Better yet, take a sample pot.

Before you come in

Bring these and the visit goes faster

  • Photos of the room — wide shots in daylight and at night, with the lights you actually use switched on.
  • A piece of what stays — flooring offcut, tile, counter sample, a cushion cover, or a cabinet door.
  • Anything you’re matching — a chip of the old colour, or a painted sample the size of your palm.
  • Rough measurements — wall area and trim lengths, so we can work out how much paint you’ll need.
